# Attribution and source boundary

Shotloom contains authored directing, design, review and editing guidance, a reference catalog, local helpers and tests. It does not bundle model weights, service code, copied manuals, personal production media or third-party film assets.

## Resource provenance and historical evidence

The five-module package has a [file-level source map](docs/baseline-map.json) and [architecture guide](docs/PUBLIC-BASELINE.md). The map records source fingerprints without machine locations. Director catalog facts retain their scoped source links; authored adaptations are labeled separately. The catalog's structure tests do not authenticate historical claims.

Model references retain their individual dated records in the [public manual register](skill/shotloom/modules/generation/references/model-manual-authority.md). Publication of this package does not mean those external pages or their demonstrations were freshly verified. Unbundled private manual evidence is explicitly identified as a gap; it is not a runtime prerequisite.

The source records include conceptual review of [DirectorSKILL](https://github.com/wuwangzhang1216/DirectorSKILL) and [DirectorAgents](https://github.com/momozi1996/DirectorAgents). The included decision-practice guidance states it is an original adaptation, not transplanted source text/templates. Their current licenses or new revisions were not assessed for this release; no license claim or runtime dependency is inferred.

The 2026-10-09 update preserves additional source records dated 2026-10-05 in the [shot-pattern cases](skill/shotloom/modules/director/references/shot-pattern-cases.md), [local execution routing](skill/shotloom/modules/edit-delivery/references/execution-routing.md), [caption conform](skill/shotloom/modules/edit-delivery/references/subtitle-conform.md) and [output-sync calibration](skill/shotloom/modules/edit-delivery/references/output-sync-calibration.md). Those guides identify conceptual references and independently authored adaptations; the timing helper and tests are maintainer-authored, not third-party runtime code. The cited external repositories, current licenses and product capabilities were not newly verified during this update. Their runtimes and tool skills are not bundled dependencies.
