# Model-neutral voice contract fixture

- Dialogue line: “店交给你了。”
- Listener and relationship: the sister is speaking to her younger brother at ordinary conversational distance
- Objective: end the farewell without revealing how reluctant she is to leave
- Loudness: quiet ordinary room volume
- Timbre or tension: steady low tension with restrained breath
- Speech rate: slightly slow, grouped as one short phrase
- Pause design: one small preparation breath before the line; no pause inside it
- Pitch and ending: one light stress on “你”, descending phrase ending
- Avoid: narration, advertisement tone, theatrical projection, crying voice, and exaggerated breathiness
