# Voice and dialogue production

Use this model-neutral layer for voice design, dialogue timing, replacement planning, and voice continuity. The maintained [sound ownership](../modules/director/references/transitions-sound.md), [audio planning](../modules/generation/references/audio-production.md), and [actual sound review](../modules/review-continuity/references/take-review.md) govern compilation and acceptance. External generation remains the user's operation.

## Separate identity from state

Maintain a stable voice passport:

- apparent age and social or regional baseline when relevant;
- timbre, resonance, baseline pitch, normal pace, and articulation;
- typical phrase ending and emotional range;
- prohibited delivery styles;
- canonical accepted sample and authorization status.

Add only the temporary state supported by the current beat. Express it through five axes: loudness, timbre or tension, speech rate and grouping, pause placement, and pitch contour or ending.

## Direct an audition

Include an exact short line, listener, conversational purpose, two or three stable voice traits, ordinary volume, pace, articulation, phrase ending, and excluded styles. Use a neutral but character-revealing line; an extreme emotional line is a poor reusable identity sample.

## Direct production dialogue

State:

- speaker and listener;
- objective, obstacle, and tactic;
- exact wording when required;
- relevant stress, if needed;
- pace, pauses, pitch contour, and ending;
- intended window relative to picture;
- story-supported temporary state;
- ambience and replacement constraints.

Do not supply only adjectives. Do not direct every word or use accent stereotypes.

## Replacement dialogue

Replace audio only when picture, performance, and mouth movement remain usable. Match actual line duration, mouth openings, pauses, emotional intensity, room acoustics, and implied microphone distance. Preserve intended acoustic continuity; do not add room tone to intentional silence. Native audio may be retained, cleaned, supplemented or replaced per element, not by a blanket rule.

Record the replacement dependency in the take review, continuity state, and handoff. A replacement route is not a reason to accept wrong speaker ownership or unusable lips.

## Review

Check identity continuity, speaker, wording, pronunciation, timing, stress, pitch ending, breath, mouth closure, room tone, effects, music, and sync. Transcription is evidence, not truth; manually verify low-confidence names, accents, and meaning-changing homophones.
