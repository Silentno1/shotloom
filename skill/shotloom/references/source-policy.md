# Source policy

Separate creative authority, model documentation, destination evidence and reviewed output. One source cannot silently substitute for another.

## Provenance and freshness

This public version uses the 2026-10-04 baseline with a scoped update synchronized on 2026-10-09. Dates inside imported references describe their source records, not new research performed during packaging or synchronization. Local checks cover structure and behavior; they do not reopen every external page or validate production quality. In particular, the 2026-10-05 review dates in the shot-pattern and post-production references are inherited records, not fresh verification by this update.

The [model manual register](../modules/generation/references/model-manual-authority.md) lists public sources and unavailable legacy material. Private documents, canvas examples, account catalogs and local project media are not bundled or required. Never claim to have read an unavailable source. Recheck consequential changing facts; otherwise mark them historical/unverified and deliver only supported planning.

Record accessibility separately from content freshness. Distinguish guest-readable, login-required, permission-restricted and not verified; a failed automated fetch proves none of the first three. The [Seedance 2.5 register](../modules/generation/references/seedance-25-sources.md) includes a scoped 2026-10-09 browser check and a non-Feishu alternative. Use bundled authored guidance with its historical date when offline; ask for a current excerpt only when an unresolved fact matters to the task.

Director cards separate scoped attributed facts, authored adaptations, countercases and unknowns. The catalog's successful structural audit does not authenticate every claim. Use only relevant read evidence; a URL alone is not proof. Project-authored methods need truthful story/design/authorization evidence, not celebrity research.

## Evidence labels

- official-current: checked current primary evidence for the exact model/version/scope.
- interface-observed: directly observed controls for the exact user destination; not inherited from this package.
- project-verified: matched inputs/settings and inspected outputs with explicit scope.
- community-observation: limited outside observations, not capability guarantees.
- unknown: unsupported or unresolved.

For historical snapshots preserve the old source date and add a freshness limitation; do not label a new task official-current merely because a reference once was. Production craft is authored method, not a volatile capability claim. User-reported settings or acceptance remain labeled reports, not independent observations.

Community discussions and secondary reporting, including Reddit and 36Kr, remain `community-observation`. Retain their observation date, exact model/operation and limits; do not use them to override official specifications or claim current model rankings. Recheck before making a consequential recommendation.

Former alpha labels surface-current/field-observed/unverified are historical vocabulary; interpret their scope explicitly instead of silently increasing confidence. Standardize new evidence only when its actual basis supports the current label.

## Reuse and rights

Retain links and attribution, not copied manuals or external proprietary assets. Public readability is not permission to redistribute expressive content. Reusable learning follows [rule learning](../modules/review-continuity/references/rule-learning-and-validation.md) only within an authorized maintenance task. One project's tolerated defect is not a universal allowance.
