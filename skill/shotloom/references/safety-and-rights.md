# Safety, rights, and consent

Shotloom provides production instructions; it does not grant rights to source material or output.

## Separate three questions

1. **Technical support:** can the selected model or surface accept this media type?
2. **Authorization:** does the user have the right and consent to use the likeness, voice, brand, music, footage, or character?
3. **Production safety:** could the requested output deceive, impersonate, exploit, or harm someone?

An upload is not proof of consent.

## Required handling

- Ask for authorization when a real person's identity or voice is central and consent is unclear.
- Prefer original characters, licensed assets, public-domain material, or user-owned references.
- Do not design prompts to imitate a living person's voice or likeness without appropriate authorization.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted characters, scenes, logos, or music merely because a model might accept the prompt.
- Do not hide synthetic provenance when disclosure is required by law, contract, or platform policy.
- Do not embed credentials, account identifiers, payment data, or private contact information in prompts or repository files.

When a protected reference is not necessary, replace it with an original description built from medium, era, palette, composition, material, camera, and motion characteristics rather than naming the protected source.
