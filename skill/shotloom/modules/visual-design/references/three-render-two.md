# 三渲二 / 3D rendered toward a 2D appearance

“三渲二” is a production/look contract, not merely a style word and not simply a post-process filter.

## Activation gate

Use the shared [activation scope](appearance-family-routing.md): a chosen/approved whole-work contract or an explicitly owned three-render-two layer/subject/interval within `mixed_media`; targeted diagnosis may inspect an existing result. Keep local rules local. Do not infer it from `anime`, `animation`, `cinematic`, `cyberpunk`, `2D`, `3D`, outlines or attractive 2D effects.

## Required dimensions

- **3D volume and perspective:** bodies and space keep coherent volume, foreshortening, contact and camera parallax.
- **Contours:** define whether outlines exist, where they thicken or disappear, and whether interior lines are selective. Outline-free work may still be 三渲二, but then shape and shadow separation must carry readability.
- **Shadow bands:** define discrete bands or controlled gradients, terminator shape, face shadow logic and highlight policy.
- **Materials:** prevent generic plastic response; state roughness, specular suppression, cloth/skin/metal separation and whether reflections are graphic or physical.
- **Texture adhesion:** painted texture must remain attached through turn, deformation and camera movement; no screen-space swimming or flashing.
- **2D effects:** state which effects are hand-drawn, graphic, screen-space or volumetric and how they interact with depth/contact.
- **Cadence:** define stepped/limited motion, pose holds, selective smoothness and camera cadence. Smooth 30fps interpolation everywhere can make the result read as game-like 3D.
- **Deformation:** define squash, stretch, smear, impact drawing, silhouette break and when volume may intentionally violate realism.

## Base lock and shot delta

The base lock stores all eight dimensions for its approved scope (the work or the owned local layer/interval). A shot delta contains only motivated changes for the current shot: emphasis, temporary suppression, action-specific cadence/deformation, effect ownership or a bounded sublook. It may not silently rewrite the base lock.

Every dimension passed to generation must be accounted for as one of: written into the prompt, owned by an approved reference, not applicable to this operation, unsupported or unverified in the active interface, or pending actual-result review. Accounting is internal; do not dump all eight dimensions into every pasteable prompt.

Select the smallest sufficient set by operation:

- quiet performance emphasizes facial volume, contour/shadow stability, material separation, texture adhesion and restrained cadence;
- spatial or moving-camera work emphasizes perspective/parallax, environment shape separation and texture adhesion through motion;
- action emphasizes pose readability, cadence contrast, deformation, silhouette break, contact-aware 2D effects and the resulting state;
- a static image carries the static appearance dimensions; cadence and deformation are included only when the image is explicitly an animation design/keyframe authority.

## Classification language

After review, choose a descriptive result rather than a binary claim:

- `3d-game-like with 2d effects`;
- `stylized 3d / cel-shaded`;
- `three-render-two, visibly 3d-led`;
- `three-render-two, close to a 2d anime appearance`;
- `primarily 2d animation with 3d assistance`.

The presence of blue/pink trails, impact cards or outlines alone does not prove the whole image is 2D-led.

## Action implications

For forceful action, do not rely on outlines alone. Combine readable support/torso mechanics, pose design, time contrast, deformation, selective camera/edit emphasis, and VFX/sound. Review whether 2D effects reveal contact or merely conceal weak body reaction.
