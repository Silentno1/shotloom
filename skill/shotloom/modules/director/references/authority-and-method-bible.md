# Authority and whole-work method bible

## Four authority layers

1. **Hard facts:** screenplay events, identities, chronology, dialogue, injuries, ownership, geography and accepted continuity.
2. **Narrative function:** what the audience must learn, feel, misread or decide; the intended progression, turn or hold and endpoint. Preserve any approved irreversible event, but do not require every atmosphere/montage insert to invent one; use the appropriate scene-direction branch.
3. **Director variables:** blocking, framing, lens, movement, performance emphasis, rhythm, sound, transition and coverage.
4. **Exact execution locks:** user-approved composition, wording, physical pose, contact, timing point, endpoint or other item that must not drift.

Every exact lock records its owner and approval basis. Model convenience cannot modify layers 1, 2 or 4.

## Whole-work method, not automatic celebrity matching

When establishing or substantially changing the whole-work method, read the complete authoritative work. For a local scene/shot adjustment, reuse its current approved method and read the relevant excerpt and dependencies; do not make new whole-work claims from that subset. Record evidence locations for conflict and moral pressure, performance scale, spatial and action needs, viewpoint, information control, rhythm, sound logic and production constraints as relevant to the requested scope. Propose a coherent method in observable terms. Named filmmakers or productions may be research provenance, but never substitute for an executable contract and never become an automatic lock from a numerical score.

Use one whole-work method bible and an approved named-reference or project-authored method before formal production, following [director-selection.md](director-selection.md). Episodes may vary intensity inside it, not silently replace its principles. A named reference needs verified scope/credit; an original project method needs truthful story/design evidence, not a celebrity identity. User selection, approval or explicit delegated selection is required; an AI score is never authorization.

## Arcane/Fortiche case-study boundary

- Fortiche and Riot describe a production based on a 2D/3D mixture.
- Pascal Charrue and Arnaud Delord are important co-founders and directing leaders, but they are not a unique permanent two-person unit that excludes other directors. Fortiche also identifies Barthélémy Maunoury as an Arcane co-director and key creative director.
- Do not store “Pascal Charrue/Arnaud Delord” as a universal indivisible authority profile.
- Candidate transfer hypotheses include actor-led animation, story-first visual invention, spatially legible action, iterative storyboard/edit thinking and mixed techniques serving dramatic function. Verify the particular adopted claim against scoped production evidence or actual sequence observations; the two general source URLs alone do not prove every method.
- Do not copy characters, cities, proprietary designs, signature shots, palette or brush treatment.

Primary references: https://forticheprod.com/faq/ and https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/riot-games-announces-new-equity-investment-in-arcane-animation-studio-fortiche-production

## Method bible fields

Before filling fields, articulate the work's specific dramatic question and desired viewer relationship: whose experience grants or denies access, what pressures recur, and how the experience changes across the work. Derive a small set of linked audiovisual decisions from that reading. For each consequential choice, state what it makes perceptible, how it may develop, and where it would be inappropriate. A list such as “cinematic, restrained, realistic” is not a method.

Test the method on contrasting existing scenes: intimate and public, quiet and forceful, early and late where present. It should distinguish them without changing its foundation. Establish a baseline and meaningful departures, not one identical camera distance, palette, acting intensity or cut rhythm for every scene. A motif can change ownership/context; do not add symbolic props or events absent from authority just to complete an arc.

The method is a decision aid, not a signature-shot quota. When local choices all comply but the episode feels generic, check whether they actually advance this work's perspective and emotional progression. Revisit the method only when authorized; preserve valid local variation and avoid mistaking a successful one-shot trick for a whole-work grammar.
