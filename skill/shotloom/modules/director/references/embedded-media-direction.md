# Embedded media direction

Treat a television, phone, monitor, game, ad or animation inside a host scene as a separate content layer.

- Tiny, defocused, unreadable content may remain background texture.
- Readable content inside the device needs a focused content asset or insert.
- When the content fills the frame or becomes an independent narrative space, it becomes a separate content unit with its own production method and generation route.

Design the boundary as an actual cut, match or hidden join. Choose how sound perspective moves between room/device playback and full-content sound: a synchronous cut, J/L overlap, crossfade, motivated continuity or deliberate silence may fit. Specify the chosen boundary and ownership, not a compulsory J/L cut. Do not demand an unverified one-take live-action-to-animation transformation merely to reduce operations.
