# Production workflow and handoff ownership

Use for whole-work preparation, a substantial sequence, or diagnosing a broken handoff. This is a dependency map, not a compulsory document chain. A local change enters at its owning stage and revisits only affected consumers. Reuse approved artifacts by version instead of copying their authority into competing bibles.

| Decision / artifact | Owner | Consumer / return condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story authority, audience experience, performance and audiovisual intent | Director | Visual, generation, edit and review; unresolved meaning returns here |
| Appearance family, assets, geometry, light/color execution | Visual design | Generation and review; incompatible design returns here |
| Unit boundaries, selected bindings, model-native operation | Generation | Review; unsupported control returns to the relevant owner, not a silent creative downgrade |
| Observed source quality, accepted state and dependency validity | Review/continuity | Edit and later generation; acceptance never means automatic full-clip use |
| Timed previs, actual selections, cut version and finishing turnover | Edit/delivery | Review reconciles selected events; changed cuts invalidate affected finishing and state approvals |

No stage approves itself on the basis of a successful tool call. A narrow answer does not activate all five skills. An upstream revision identifies the source version, changed decision, preserved locks and affected consumers; unchanged work remains valid.

## Shared contracts: one authority, scoped consumers

Director identity, evidence-kind vocabulary and lock inheritance belong to [director-selection.md](director-selection.md); appearance activation belongs to [appearance-family-routing.md](../../visual-design/references/appearance-family-routing.md); media version/range and dependency validity belong to [continuity-ledger.md](../../review-continuity/references/continuity-ledger.md); cut timing and event disposition belong to [narrative-contexts.md](../../review-continuity/references/narrative-contexts.md). Model-native grammar stays in the generation adapters and their official-manual authority.

The private helper [drama_contracts.py](../scripts/drama_contracts.py) shares primitive checks; it is not a sixth workflow. Required descriptive decisions need meaningful text: bare `false`, `0`, whitespace or `[null]` cannot stand for a visual, sound or review decision. Legitimate zero timing, boolean controls and explicit absence remain valid in their typed fields or inside a description such as “no contour; edges are separated by tone.” Structure never proves evidence authenticity or quality.

Reuse existing versioned artifacts; do not create a new compulsory form. New source-backed acceptance retains a SHA-256 and valid selected range when present. Declaring a hash is not verifying the file. Legacy unbound evidence remains visibly unbound until actually checked; do not backfill invented hashes or automatically migrate projects. Recheck affected consumers and actual source versions, not unrelated artifacts. A catalog revision alone neither reselects the project's director nor authorizes production.

Formal production readiness also requires the current [director-style selection](director-selection.md). All five stages inherit the same lock ID/version/content binding and current script authority. Before selection, only explicitly non-production analysis, comparison and exploratory previs are eligible; a paid exploratory test needs separate scoped generation authorization. Do not silently promote its output to formal production or resume a paused project. Stale/missing selections return to director selection, not to an invented platform preset.

## Preparation: allocate effort to the work's real risks

From the complete approved scope, identify the few sequences whose failure would undermine the work: a relationship turn, reveal, action geography, recurring environment, exact dialogue/contact, or a distinctive visual/sound event. Rank by dramatic importance, uncertainty, reuse and downstream dependency—not by shot count or spectacle alone.

For each material risk, decide the cheapest sufficient evidence: existing approved material, a paper continuity check, timed boards, a spatial mock-up, a temporary voice reading, or an authorized model test. A test requires a discriminating question and observable result; do not generate a montage of attractive images as proof of sustained acting. Establish essential assets/control inputs before dependent generations. Batch reusable neutral assets where appropriate, but choose actual scene staging before treating an identity sheet as shot coverage.

Plan coverage and source handles for the intended edit and downstream sound/VFX needs. Reserve time/attempts within existing authorization for quality-critical work; do not invent a fixed credit budget, silently lower standards, reset retry counts, or spend to complete a planning form. If requirements cannot coexist within the allowed resources, expose that tradeoff before submission.

Identify destination-dependent constraints early only when they affect composition, text, sound, clearance or deliverables. Route their verification to [release-and-compliance.md](../../edit-delivery/references/release-and-compliance.md). Unknown distribution choices remain assumptions, not invented platform rules.

## Previsualization as an editorial test

Use a timed assembly when interaction, narrative timing, complex space, sound-led structure or expensive dependencies cannot be judged from individual panels. Existing boards, simple blocking diagrams, white-model views or rough stills may suffice; polished assets and new generation are not prerequisites. Route assembly to [previsualization and edit phases](../../edit-delivery/references/director-editing.md).

Give it the intended scene duration, temporary exact dialogue or an explicitly non-final reading, material sound cues and meaningful transitions. Test: does the viewer understand the intended relationship; is the reveal timed correctly; can movement occupy the space; does silence/overlap breathe; does the planned duration contain dead travel or unreadable compression? Revise the responsible staging, coverage or timing decision before committing dependent shots. A boards-only timing pass validates design, not model performance, lip sync or physical execution.

Skip a separate animatic for simple well-evidenced local work. Reuse valid previs after unaffected changes. Do not turn temporary voices/music into release assets or authorize uploads/paid tests by calling them previs.

## Readiness and return paths

- Ready to compile: director-style selection and inheritance valid, consequential creative choices resolved, selected references valid for their roles, execution route evidenced, review target clear. Unimportant optional detail may remain open.
- Ready to edit: source inspection and selected range evidence available; source acceptance does not settle the final cut.
- Ready to finish: cut and representative soundtrack establish the approved experience; selected events/state reviewed; turnover versions match.
- Ready to deliver: actual export verified against the named destination, scoped exceptions explicit. Publishing remains separate authorization.

If locally correct shots make a flat scene, return to scene/episode organization rather than endlessly intensifying prompts. If the organization works but a take fails a named requirement, repair that control or take. If an approved edit changes events, go back through narrative authority and continuity before calling it a technical fix.
