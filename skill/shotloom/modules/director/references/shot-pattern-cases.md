# Conditional shot-pattern cases

Use only to make a consequential shot choice concrete or compare alternatives. These are authored directing examples, not footage observations, named-director evidence or model-tested prompts. They do not select a director, change story facts or impose a look. Inherit the current method bible and appearance contract; consult [shot-camera-time-design.md](shot-camera-time-design.md) for the underlying distinctions.

## Adapt the relationship, not the recipe

For a relevant case carry only: intended audience effect; necessary starting relationship; visible development and event anchor; exit/cut relation; legitimate alternative; likely failure and evidence needed. Put the choices in the existing shot contract, not another mandatory card. Choose duration from the event, performance and edit. A code demo's frame numbers, pixel distances, camera angles or easing curves are not video-model controls.

### 1. Approach that changes attention / 靠近与注意力

- **When useful:** the viewer should gradually inspect a known person or detail more closely, or feel a chosen reduction in distance while an unresolved situation persists.
- **Design:** name the initial readable context and the target that gains emphasis. Let an actual glance, hesitation, disclosure or continuing pressure motivate the progression; decide whether the approach stops, continues into the cut or is interrupted. Moving camera, optical zoom, crop and a character approaching have different consequences.
- **Alternative/countercase:** a fixed shared frame may preserve an important listener or let pressure accumulate without movement. A close-up can also withhold information rather than reveal it. Do not apply “tense = slow push.”
- **Failure/review:** does the approach erase the other person, hand or clue carrying the beat? Does the camera outrun the performance? Inspect the whole move and its cut at normal playback, not only the final attractive frame.

### 2. Reveal by changing spatial access / 改变可见范围

- **When useful:** an already-authorized relationship is initially hidden by framing or occlusion and becomes available to the viewer.
- **Design:** establish the starting anchor, what hides the information, when it becomes legible, and the relevant reaction or exit. A pullback, lateral move, opening obstruction, focus transfer or cut can reveal different kinds of information; choose one that actually exposes the target.
- **Alternative/countercase:** showing the relationship from the start can create suspense; withholding it can create surprise. Neither is intrinsically superior. Flat 2D layering can reveal by overlap without requiring 3D parallax or photographic depth of field.
- **Failure/review:** a newly visible object must not be invented solely to justify a reveal. Check world placement and identity before/after occlusion, then whether the audience has time and contrast to understand what was revealed. A later close-up may clarify, but is not compulsory.

### 3. Attention transfer inside a shared frame / 同框中的主次交接

- **When useful:** a listener, background action or shared task changes the meaning while the current speaker remains present.
- **Design:** identify who owns attention first, the stimulus that transfers it, and how blocking, eyeline, contrast, focus or restrained reframing makes the transfer readable. Preserve the other person's continuing behavior when it matters.
- **Alternative/countercase:** a cut to a reaction can isolate information unavailable in the shared frame. Deep focus or graphic emphasis may fit better than a rack focus. No obligatory gesture, blink, camera move or cut per line.
- **Failure/review:** two competing peaks, a listener looking at the wrong target, or an insert that loses the relationship can destroy the effect. Judge voice, body and attention together; do not reward merely including both faces.

### 4. Accent within an action phrase / 动作句内的重音

- **When useful:** an approved strike, evasion, release or other state-changing event needs a perceptible accent within surrounding movement.
- **Design:** locate preparation, decisive event and consequence; choose which carries the emphasis. Acceleration/arrest, a pose hold, selective time expansion, framing, graphic treatment or sound may support that event according to the approved medium. Do not stretch one hit to fill an available generation slot.
- **Alternative/countercase:** continuous normal-speed action can be stronger than a pause or camera shake. A held image with moving effects is not the same as freezing the whole scene; stepped drawing cadence is not automatically slow motion. An approved offscreen impact can be conveyed by established cause and aftermath, not fabricated visible contact.
- **Failure/review:** inspect body/tool/power causality before adding an effect. Retimed picture cannot put recoil before the hit or blindly stretch speech. Verify the accent at normal playback and disputed contact in source-timed frames. Read [stylized-action.md](stylized-action.md) when actually directing action; this case does not replace it.

### 5. Conclude or carry motion through the cut / 落定与带势切出

- **When useful:** a gesture, move or reveal needs either a readable consequence or momentum into the next shot.
- **Design:** decide what the viewer must register by the exit and what remains in motion. A hold can expose a changed relationship; a match on action or sound bridge can carry energy forward. Size the readable interval for the actual information and performance, not a fixed logo/product hold.
- **Alternative/countercase:** an abrupt interruption, unresolved ending, stillness or deliberate repeated framing can serve the chosen method. No mandatory settled landing, reversal, fade, once-only technique or shot-count quota.
- **Failure/review:** a technically clean trim can remove the payoff, and a decorative hold can drain urgency. Review the actual outgoing and incoming ranges with representative sound; the last source frame alone does not prove a good ending.

## Execution boundary and provenance

Generation translates the selected relationship through the exact model/mode's official guidance and supported references. Post-production can execute approved graphic layers or retiming only when the source supports them; route concrete tool choices through [post execution routing](../../edit-delivery/references/execution-routing.md). Do not make graphic-demo precision or a simulated camera move a promise about generated character acting.
