# Shot scale, camera and screen-time design

Use for selecting or revising 景别、机位、运镜、焦点、升格/慢动作 and related time treatment. This is a director decision guide, not a list to paste into every prompt. Story, appearance and exact execution locks remain authoritative. The options below are craft choices, not mandatory shot orders, emotional formulas or platform controls.

## Start with the visible job

Identify what the viewer needs to perceive now, whose access to information matters, what is intentionally withheld, and what changes or persists by the exit. Choose framing, blocking, movement and time together to make that relationship readable. A held wide shot can be intimate; a close-up can conceal geography; spectacular motion may serve sensation rather than reveal new plot information. Do not invent a story event to justify a technique.

Use the smallest sufficient shot description. Carry its function, consequential spatial/temporal choices, exact locks and allowed variation in the existing director contract; no new form is required. If an approved tiny eye cue is unreadable in a locked distant composition, flag the conflict rather than silently strengthening the acting or changing the shot.

## Shot scale and coverage / 景别

Choose for the required information at the intended display size, not the emotion's name. Framing terminology varies; describe the relevant visible body extent, occupied frame area and surrounding anchors when precision matters.

| Option | Useful decision | Guard against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extreme wide / 远景、大远景 | Make distance, isolation, group pattern or environment relation legible | Expecting a tiny facial cue to carry the beat; adding a compulsory establishing shot when geography is already known |
| Wide / full-body / 全景 | Read posture, foot support, approach, contact and shared space | Cropping away the only causal relationship merely to enlarge a face |
| Medium / 中景 | Combine face, hands, task and interaction | Defaulting every exchange to isolated frontal talking portraits |
| Medium close / close / 近景、特写 | Concentrate on a reaction, attention shift, detail or withheld context | Treating closeness as automatic emotion; losing the gesture or listener needed for meaning |
| Extreme close / insert / 大特写、细节插入 | Isolate evidence, texture, sensation, motif or a decisive detail | Inventing details; confusing an insert's function with one compulsory framing size |

Two-shots, group shots, over-shoulders, point-of-view and reaction shots describe relationships/functions, not separate scale grades. Choose whether a shared frame, a transfer within it, a cutaway or a shot/reverse-shot better carries this beat. A speaker change does not mandate a cut. No compulsory wide-to-medium-to-close ladder, angle grid, shot-size quota or minimum cut frequency.

## Viewpoint, composition, optics and focus / 机位与焦点

- Set the meaningful camera side, height and viewing direction relative to the subjects and established space. Eye-level, low, high, overhead or canted views may change access, vulnerability, scale or subjective balance; none has one fixed emotional meaning. A low camera and an upward tilt are different decisions.
- Use frame occupancy, head/look/movement room, negative space, edge cropping, depth layers and occlusion only as they serve the event. Centered symmetry, asymmetry and flat graphic staging are all valid. Keep required hands, faces, contact or evidence readable somewhere in the coverage, not necessarily all at once in every frame.
- Viewpoint/distance determines perspective. At a fixed viewpoint, changing focal length primarily changes field of view/magnification; the familiar wider-near versus longer-far perspective difference also involves changing camera distance. Do not promise new parallax from zooming, or assign an exact focal length/sensor configuration without a reason and applicable control.
- Decide whether relevant layers should stay readable together or attention should transfer through focus. For a rack focus / 移焦, identify the current and next target, trigger and important overlap; focus change does not require camera travel. Preserve intended ambiguity, deep focus, flat 2D clarity or partial defocus. Do not impose shallow depth of field on every style or every close shot.
- Track eyelines, screen direction and access across cuts. A motivated axis crossing, a neutral view or a continuous reorientation may re-establish geography; do not turn the axis into an absolute ban or excuse an accidental reversal with a label.

## Movement selection / 运镜

First consider whether blocking, focus, a cut or a fixed frame already delivers the desired effect. Movement is optional. Common choices are below; describe uncommon or ambiguous terms by their visible consequence.

| Mechanism | What changes / potential use | Distinguish from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locked frame / 固定 | Subject entry, exit, distance or obstruction develops against stable boundaries; can sustain pressure or comedy | Accidental drift; fixed position with a deliberate pan or zoom is not a fully locked frame |
| Dolly in/out / 推、拉 | Camera translates toward/away; alters viewer-subject distance and parallax, may concentrate or reveal | Lens zoom, digital crop, subject approaching the camera |
| Pan / tilt / 摇、俯仰 | Camera rotates horizontally/vertically from its position to follow, search or reveal | Lateral travel or physical elevation |
| Track / follow / 移、跟 | Camera travels relative to subject or space; specify relative distance and anchors | The whole environment sliding with the subject; requiring constant speed or distance in every follow |
| Pedestal / crane / boom / 升降、摇臂 | Camera changes elevation or follows an arc, revealing depth/relations | 升格; tilting upward alone |
| Orbit / 环绕 | Camera changes viewing side around an anchor; plan what becomes visible and how geography changes | A subject turn or a decorative mandatory climax move |
| Zoom / 变焦 | Field of view changes from the same viewpoint; may search, isolate or stylize | Dolly translation; new physical parallax |
| Dolly zoom / 移动变焦 | Translation and compensating focal change can retain subject size while background relation shifts | A fixed camera plus a contradictory travel command |
| Whip / handheld / roll | A motivated rapid reorientation, operator-like instability or camera-axis rotation | Universal impact shake or an obligation to make all action unstable |

A compound move can combine mechanisms coherently; specify phase order only when it actually changes. Name consequential start/reveal/exit relations rather than choreographing every centimeter. Match lead/lag, acceleration, pause or reversal to the beat and approved medium. A move may settle, continue into a cut, lose a subject intentionally or be interrupted; no compulsory stable landing. Deliberate disorientation needs an intended perceptual effect and a readable recovery only when the scene requires one.

## Time treatment / 升格、变速与停顿

Keep separate: story-event duration, intended on-screen duration, generation clip duration, source/output frame rate, and animation cadence. A longer clip is not automatically slow motion; fewer unique drawings or pose holds are not automatically time dilation. Traditional overcranking / 升格 produces slow playback by capturing faster than playback; undercranking / 降格 does the converse. Neither is a vertical camera move. AI can synthesize the visible effect without implementing those capture settings; a prompt's fps number is not a verified frame-rate control.

| Treatment | Specify when it matters | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal-speed continuous action / 常速 | Readable event progression, natural or stylized acceleration and pauses | Flattening everything to constant eased speed; confusing a character's cautious movement with slow playback |
| Slow motion / 慢动作、升格效果 | Why this interval deserves expanded attention; entry event, affected action/layers, exit or continued slow passage | Stretching the entire attack/reaction to fill available seconds; requiring a return to normal when the whole approved shot is slow |
| Speed ramp / 变速 | Ordered normal/slow/fast regions, transition anchor and endpoint; ratio only if it is a real lock | Treating camera acceleration alone as a playback-speed change; changing duration without reconciling the cut |
| Fast motion / 快动作、降格效果 | What elapsed action is compressed and which meaningful states survive | Making a locked spoken line unintelligible; substituting rapid movement for narrative progression |
| Time-lapse / 延时 | Which longer-term change is sampled/compressed and which spatial anchors persist | Claiming a short source contains elapsed events it never recorded; confusing with a short action simply played faster |
| Freeze / pose hold / 定格、停顿 | Whole image frozen, character pose held with other layers moving, or a designed impact accent; entry and release | Treating these as interchangeable, or judging all limited-animation holds as accidental frozen output |
| Bullet-time-like treatment / 时间凝滞绕拍 | Whether the world freezes/slows while viewpoint moves; reveal, readable suspended state and resumption if needed | Assuming a physical rig, multiview reconstruction or a model feature is available because the term is written |

For any material time transformation, identify its audience function, temporal scope and affected layers: bodies, camera, props, particles and audio need not all share the same clock if the approved stylization deliberately separates them. Otherwise preserve causal synchronization; a slowed fist cannot hit an already recoiling body by accident. An event anchor such as release, realization or contact is often more useful than arbitrary decimals. Exact timing remains an explicit lock when required, not a guarantee of model compliance.

Sound ownership does not change with picture speed. Decide whether native dialogue remains at normal timing, is transformed, yields to a designed subjective layer, or is handled in post under the existing sound plan. Do not automatically stretch dialogue, music or every effect with picture; do not add post-only sounds to the generation request. Mouth timing, impact transients and music/edit anchors must be rechecked after any retime.

## Choose where the effect is made, then judge it in sequence

For rhythm decisions, distinguish actor speed, speech/pauses, motion within the frame, cut frequency, story-to-screen time and music pulse. They need not rise together. An urgent action can remain in a readable held frame; a still wait can be cut rapidly if attention genuinely alternates. Select the event that changes scale, movement, cutting or time treatment, and name the loss in a simpler alternative when it matters. No default “tense = fast cutting,” “sad = close-up” or fixed source length. For director-specific alternatives and voice/body coordination, use [director-decision-practice.md](director-decision-practice.md).

Director design names the visible target. Generation decides how a supported reference, native prompt or actual control can carry it; edit decides whether inspected source media can be retimed within authorization. Do not promise either route before checking its constraints. Smooth slow motion cannot be inferred from output fps alone; post interpolation can invent artifacts around occlusion/contact, and intended stepped animation should not be automatically smoothed.

Read the planned shots together: attention transfer, screen direction, scale contrast or deliberate repetition, movement at the join, time continuity or intentional ellipsis, and sound bridge. A slow interval may emphasize a glance, falling object, landscape sensation or action; it is not a combat-only device or a mandatory mark of quality. Choose the actual cut duration from effect and available material, not a seconds-per-shot table.

Pass only consequential choices to compilation and review. Check the returned sequence at intended playback speed and display size, then inspect dense source frames where needed. Separate design clarity, model execution and editorial fit. A good still cannot verify a move, focus transition, ramp, impact accent or emotional timing. Unsupported precision remains a disclosed risk; no paid test, new asset, changed lock or lower standard is authorized by this guide.

For a worked comparison or when an abstract technique name is not enough to specify the intended effect, use [shot-pattern-cases.md](shot-pattern-cases.md). Its conditional cases are authored design examples, not fixed durations, required coverage or verified model recipes.
