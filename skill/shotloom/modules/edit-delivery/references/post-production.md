# Post planning, finishing and reconform

Plan and test consequential post needs before generation/picture lock: sound ownership and sync, VFX plates/occlusions, handles, color/reference consistency, exact text and delivery variants. A small feasibility test may change capture/control requirements. Final conform, mix and mastering use the locked cut; useful sound design, cleanup and look development may begin earlier without being declared final. Representative temporary sound that affects editorial judgment belongs in the pre-lock rough cut.

Prepare dialogue edit and cleanup, ambience/room tone, Foley and impact design, music edit, loudness target for the actual platform, color consistency, approved text/subtitles, graphics, accessibility variants, master/export settings and QC.

Read [subtitle-conform.md](subtitle-conform.md) for creating, correcting or reconforming captions, and [output-sync-calibration.md](output-sync-calibration.md) for consequential dialogue/contact/music synchronization or suspected export drift. For a concrete tool/layer choice use [execution-routing.md](execution-routing.md). These are conditional work methods, not three extra gates on every edit.

Start from the reviewed per-element treatment plan. Use a hybrid soundtrack when it gives the best result: keep comfortable, synchronized native sound; clean it only as needed; supplement missing layers; replace defective layers; and preserve motivated silence. Never impose a blanket rule that all generated audio must be kept or all of it must be recreated.

Build scene-level ambience only where the scene requires it. For scenes assembled from several generated clips, keep acoustic identity continuous while allowing motivated perspective changes. Post-owned crowd murmur, room tone, weather, machinery, score, narration, recurring UI cues or other layers are added according to the approved ownership plan, not merely because the track feels empty. Avoid looping intelligible background speech that exposes repetition.

Do not use post-production to conceal an unresolved story fact, broken identity, false contact or missing endpoint unless the user explicitly approves the editorial repair.

Keep picture, clean dialogue/effects, music and caption masters separately when practical. Record version and checksum for final deliverables.

## Turnover and approved cut changes

Bind turnover to a cut version/hash with reference picture/timebase, source-to-timeline ranges, retime map, handles, sound IDs/ownership/treatment, text/caption versions and pending exceptions. Include only needed deliverables; do not require a studio interchange format unsupported by the chosen tools. Give VFX/color/audio enough source and references to preserve their role without changing story or look authority.

An authorized post-lock change creates a new cut version, not a silent overwrite. Diff affected fragments/events and identify affected VFX, grade, titles/captions, lip/contact sync, music/edit anchors, ambience bridges and exports. Reuse unchanged work; reconform changed sections against the new picture, then review joins and the complete final playback. Prior final QC no longer covers the changed export.

If finishing exposes insufficient handles, impossible text timing, missing plates or unrecoverable dialogue, return that specific dependency to edit/generation/director. Do not disguise it with unrelated motion, crops, invented sound or a lowered acceptance target. A regeneration proposal still obeys current pause/cost/attempt authority.
