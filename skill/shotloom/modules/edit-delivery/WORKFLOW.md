# AI Drama Edit and Delivery

Formal cutting, picture lock and creative finishing inherit the current authorized director-style selection and artifact `director_style_ref` under [director-selection.md](../director/references/director-selection.md). Check new/changed handoffs with `../director/scripts/director_style.py check PACKET --stage edit`; missing/stale locks allow diagnostic recommendations or explicitly exploratory previs, not formal creative approval. Preserve its audience viewpoint, time treatment, rhythm and sound logic while varying intensity for the actual scene. A purely technical inspection/export of an unchanged already approved cut does not retroactively reselect a director or authorize a remake. Reuse approved choices, do not impose generic trailer pacing.

## Select the phase

- **Previsualization:** assemble available boards/blocking with provisional timing and representative temporary dialogue/sound to test design before generation. Clearly distinguish designed timing from actual source timecodes and do not claim execution quality from boards.

- **Clip selection/cut adjustment:** inspect the relevant source and neighboring picture/sound dependencies; return reproducible ranges or the requested edit. Do not start post-production or platform research automatically.
- **Rough-cut review/picture lock:** review the complete candidate cut with representative temporary sound. Preserve the normal, silent and audio-only passes below before picture lock; a local fix alone cannot prove the whole cut is ready.
- **Post planning/tests:** anticipate required sound, VFX, color, text, handles and turnover before generation/lock when they affect feasibility. **Final post-production:** use the established picture lock and approved treatment plan. **Packaging/delivery:** read current requirements only for the named destination; packaging does not authorize publication.
- Reuse valid metadata and prior checks for unchanged files/settings. Recheck changed regions and dependencies; a new final export still needs technical verification and relevant audiovisual review.
- A scoped approved edit includes its necessary preview, repair and export steps; do not ask again for each. Stop for new cost, uploads/publication, unapproved destructive overwrites, or material changes to creative locks. Preserve source media. Task handoffs go to the user-selected location; EDLs and renders stay in their authorized output location.

## Workflow (select only the phase needed)

1. Establish the phase and authoritative inputs. For actual editing, establish actual source files, duration, frame rate/time base and review status; previs uses the identified boards/layouts with explicitly provisional timing. Request clarification only when a material choice is unresolved.
   When selecting or changing a concrete editing/compositing tool, use [references/execution-routing.md](references/execution-routing.md); reuse an adequate existing route. A discovered skill is not proof its runtime is ready or permission to install, upload or generate.
2. For selection and cut design, use [references/director-editing.md](references/director-editing.md) to separate technical usability from dramatic selection.
3. Produce reproducible picture/audio in/out points, handles, transition logic and reasons. Never infer timecodes from a prompt or nominal duration.
   For material trims, splits, replacements or reordering, reconcile retained events and state through [selected-cut continuity](../review-continuity/references/narrative-contexts.md). Editing proposes the delta; it does not approve canon.
4. When reviewing a rough cut for picture lock, build a pre-lock temporary soundtrack for every sound that can affect cut rhythm, intelligibility, continuity or dramatic hierarchy, including temporary versions of planned `post_only` layers. Review the rough cut normally, silently and audio-only. Lock picture only after story, performance, space, rhythm and the representative temporary soundtrack survive those passes.
5. For post planning, turnover, finishing or approved post-lock changes, use [references/post-production.md](references/post-production.md). It routes caption work to precise source-to-cut conform and consequential audio-sync work to final-output calibration. Replace temporary layers only under the approved treatment plan and revalidate affected work after a cut change.
6. For packaging or delivery, separate creative packaging from platform compliance using [references/release-and-compliance.md](references/release-and-compliance.md). Verify the actual platform and date when making compliance claims.

## Rules

- An accepted generation is only eligible source; it is not automatically used in full.
- One file may supply multiple fragments or none. Several files may form one beat.
- A cut point must name the dramatic, motion, gaze, graphic or sound reason.
- Native sound is eligible source, not a default master and not disposable by default. Decide per element whether to retain, clean, supplement, replace or intentionally omit it.
- Preserve the whole-work method and visual bible; editing cannot silently replace them with a generic trailer rhythm.
- Creative cover/title/description decisions are not delivery compliance.
- Do not hard-code upload category, aspect ratio, duration, codec, caption or moderation rules unless current official or interface evidence supports them.
