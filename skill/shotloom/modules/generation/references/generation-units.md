# Generation-unit planning

## Goal

Find the largest unit that remains causally clear, controllable, reviewable and compatible with one reference structure. Do not minimize operation count at the expense of fidelity, and do not split merely because the framing changes. For a model-native plan, use the selected documented operation's envelope; apply the actual host envelope when binding an execution path. A third-party ordinary endpoint must not erase a documented ultra-long/sequence branch from planning.

## Keep together when

- place and time are continuous;
- identity, wardrobe, props, space and generation-owned sound authorities are compatible;
- actions form one causal chain;
- internal cuts can be described absolutely;
- the endpoint remains reviewable;
- duration fits the selected documented operation, and the actual host when preparing submission.

## Split when

- references require incompatible roles or modes;
- a precise first/last frame or UI/text insert needs its own control path;
- the story crosses time, space or medium without a controllable transition/continuity bridge in the chosen operation;
- a critical contact or subtle performance repeatedly fails while competing with spectacle;
- the selected operation's limit is reached; at execution, a narrower host requires an authorized path/boundary decision rather than silently changing the model-native plan;
- a motivated full-frame carrier creates a clean join.

These are control/review reasons, not automatic fragmentation rules. A documented multi-stage or ultra-long mode can include planned scene changes; preserve its sequence-level state, reference ownership and transition checks. A longer mode is an option, not a target, and does not override a brief that requires independently controlled shots.

## Dense-phase budget

For each phase identify primary invariant, precision owner, support method, allowed approximation and forbidden substitution. Camera, contact, distributed environment change, subtle performance and large VFX are separate high-risk owners; do not demand all as equal priorities in one instant.

No fixed hit duration exists. A generation can contain several exchanges, while the edit may retain only fractions of it.

A scene-level `post_only` ambience bed does not by itself require adjacent pictures to be generated together. It may instead bridge otherwise valid units in the edit. Conversely, incompatible `native_required` dialogue, contact or acoustic perspective may justify a different control path or unit boundary.

Unit planning inherits the approved [preparation priorities and previs](../../director/references/production-workflow.md). Choose the actual operation duration for useful action plus necessary edit handles, not the desired final cut length alone. Reuse approved assets and compatible successful takes without treating them as mandatory coverage. A generation can contain multiple useful fragments; track their selection separately from source acceptance. Resolve an upstream dramatic/spatial gap before spending retries on wording.
