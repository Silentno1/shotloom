# Standalone audio production

Use for voice/ADR, narration, Foley, effects, ambience or music planning and local authorized replacement. External sound generation is performed by the user, not this package. Director owns dramatic use, exact words, voice identity and ownership; edit owns timeline/sync and treatment; review owns actual listening and acceptance.

## Source lock and operation boundary

Use `media_type: audio` with the common authority, production level, reference manifest, `required_endpoint` and `review_criteria`. Do not require an appearance family or camera plan. Add `audio_brief` with `purpose`, `delivery_role` (`guide` or `master_candidate`), `sound_ids`, `content`, `timing`, `voice_or_source_identity`, `acoustic_perspective`, and `usage_authority`. These are project fields, not proprietary API syntax. `timing` can name event anchors, free timing or exact approved picture ranges; unknown material sync stays unresolved.

Carry exact dialogue/lyrics when authorized, speaker/voice identity, pronunciation, phrase intent and allowed variation. For non-speech, identify source/material/action or musical function without inventing dialogue. State when identity is not applicable (e.g. a noise bed), rather than requiring a fictional voice. Reference approval and provenance still apply; voice/music use or reference uploads need appropriate authorization. A timing guide with restricted rights is not release-cleared.

Keep the original picture sound partition unchanged. `post_only` excludes its audible master from picture generation, but does not forbid this separately authorized audio job. The standalone packet names sound IDs and destination; it does not relabel them `native_required` to bypass a checker. Do not attach a picture-generation sound partition to the audio lock; link its authoritative version instead.

## Compile for the actual audio interface

Verify the chosen tool's current voice/reference, language, pronunciation, duration, output/stem, editing and rights-relevant controls before relying on them. There is no presumed universal audio adapter. Use exact official/interface evidence; an H3/Seedance video prompt is not an audio-service prompt. Without a verified adapter, deliver a clearly labeled audio brief or identify the unsupported control; do not claim a validated executable prompt.

Choose native retained material, local editing, separately generated audio or licensed/user-supplied assets according to the defect and approved treatment. Do not replace good dialogue/contact just because post gives more control. If a fixed picture cannot accommodate exact words, return the timing conflict to edit/director rather than silently rewriting or rushing speech.

## Review and turnover

Track version/hash, linked sound IDs, source/cut version and actual duration. Audition alone and in picture/neighboring sound: exact wording, identity, expressive development, breath/phrasing, artifacts, intelligibility, sync, acoustic perspective and dramatic hierarchy. A transcript or waveform cannot approve these alone. A master candidate is not accepted simply because it generated.

Edit owns placement, handles, fades, looping/crossfades, routing/stems and actual delivery format. Inspect loop seams and repeated intelligible chatter; preserve intentional silence. Guides are visibly labeled and excluded from final masters unless separately approved and cleared. A changed cut invalidates affected timing/placement, not necessarily the underlying sound asset. Preserve versioned sources and return scoped failures to the responsible owner.
