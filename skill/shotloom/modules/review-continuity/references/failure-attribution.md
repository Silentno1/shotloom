# Failure attribution and retry

Before treating a finding as a generation failure, apply the disposition gate in [review-coverage-and-disposition.md](review-coverage-and-disposition.md). Separate a detected tolerable imperfection, a repairable local defect, a failure requiring affected-unit regeneration and insufficient review evidence. Missing coverage calls for better inspection, not automatic regeneration. A planned edit is not an accomplished fix; re-review the changed result and affected boundaries. General maintenance never resets attempts or reopens accepted material.

Classify the principal cause:

1. story/scene contract;
2. director design;
3. production method/final look;
4. asset or spatial authority;
5. prompt compilation/platform adapter;
6. stochastic model execution;
7. edit/post-production.

Record symptom, violated authority, evidence, primary cause, confidence as plain high/medium/low, one changed variable, invariants, expected observable improvement and escalation condition.

For a video chosen under [task-based model selection](../../generation/references/video-model-selection.md), check whether the declared advantage actually survived into the inspected result. A wrong input binding, lost prompt cue, host transform or unreviewed soundtrack does not establish a model weakness. A matched failure may justify reassessing the exact mode/control or candidate, not a blanket brand downgrade. A predeclared fallback is conditional diagnosis only: preserve creative locks, verify the alternative addresses this failure, and respect the project's authorized attempts and pause. If post-production can repair only the failed sound layer while preserving approved picture/sync, do not switch video models just to exercise the fallback.

## Trace the earliest supported loss

For a broad complaint such as generic, lifeless or factory-made, inspect representative contrasting beats rather than treating the entire work as one defect. Trace the intended audience effect and its concrete carrier through director plan, selected reference/control, actual submitted operation, returned media and chosen edit. Locate the earliest evidenced loss; keep unavailable links unknown. Distinguish an inadequate design, an ignored existing instruction, an unsupported control and an editorial compromise. A rule that already addresses the defect needs execution correction, not a synonymous new rule.

Include an apparent success or legitimate countercase. If a wrist-light interaction survives from plan to media, do not call that beat evidence that every prompt lost bodily acting. If spatially correct framing still fails the approved audience effect, a technical pass is not a creative pass. Do not infer a whole work's artistic quality from a few stills, or a model's limits from one take. Reusable maintenance needs a demonstrated gap in the skill, not merely a disappointing outcome.

## Retry packet

For performance defects, distinguish an absent/contradictory director target, a lost or exaggerated compiled cue, observed model non-execution, and coverage/edit hiding an otherwise present response. Use source-to-prompt-to-media evidence; without a matching source/prompt, keep causal attribution tentative. Do not call every lifeless result a model limit or treat every defect with more intense emotion words. A local correction may target gaze, intensity, response timing or bodily coordination while preserving successful identity and other acting. If the overall performance design is incoherent, rebuild that design instead of micromanaging one muscle at a time.

After inspecting the actual result, choose between local repair and upstream rebuild.

- **Local repair:** the source contract is sound. Record one principal `change_scope`, `locked_successes`, the target adapter or control method, expected observable improvement and stopping condition.
- **Upstream rebuild:** story beat, blocking, topology, camera logic, visual authority or required endpoint is structurally wrong. Route the failure to its owner and rebuild the generation snapshot; do not hide it with more adjectives.
- **Capability/control mismatch:** the active model or interface cannot reliably isolate the change. Escalate to a focused reference, keyframe, mask/region tool, smaller unit, another platform or an editorial solution.

The retry packet may preserve an observed success without adding it to canon. Canon changes only after the take is explicitly accepted.

For an approved `three_render_two` family, attribute a local visual failure to one principal dimension: volume/perspective, contours, shadow bands, materials, texture adhesion, 2D effects, cadence or deformation. Preserve the other observed successes. Do not use this dimension list for photographic, hand-drawn 2D, standard 3D, stop-motion, motion-graphics or mixed-media work unless that family independently defines the same concern.

Do not respond to every failure by lengthening the prompt. After two materially different attempts reproduce the same failure, change control method, reference authority, platform, unit boundary or editorial plan.

For sound, do not retry picture generation because a planned `post_only` layer is absent. Route a removable or replaceable audio defect to edit/post-production when the picture and synchronization-critical material remain usable. Retry generation only when a required embodied performance or contact relationship cannot be repaired without losing the take; preserve any native sound that already passes.

When this diagnosis is explicitly being generalized into reusable guidance or a skill repair, apply [rule-learning-and-validation.md](rule-learning-and-validation.md). A plausible suggested rewrite is not a verified fix; a local failure is not proof of a universal model limitation. This route grants no additional write or generation permission.
